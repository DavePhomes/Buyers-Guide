Buying a home comes with a long list of things people don't say out loud.

Am I overpaying? What am I not seeing that I should be? Is this even the right time, or am I about to make a mistake I'll be explaining to myself in five years?

Those aren't small questions, and you deserve real answers, not a sales pitch.

In my experience working with buyers, the most common thing I hear years after closing isn't regret about the house itself. It's buyers wishing they'd thought about something sooner, asked a different question, or understood a tradeoff before they were already committed. Not because anyone failed them. Because nobody made sure they were thinking far enough ahead.

That's the difference I try to make. My job isn't just to get you to closing. It's to make sure you arrive there having actually considered everything, so the home you buy today still works for you years from now.

The Process, At a Glance

Here's the shape of it

Buying a home has a lot of moving parts, and most of them are new to first-time buyers and rusty for repeat ones. Once we sit down together, we'll go through each step in real detail and build a plan around your specific situation, timeline, and goals.

Get Pre-Approved

Before we look at a single home, we get you pre-approved with a lender who fits your situation. This tells you what you can actually afford and tells sellers you're serious the moment we find the right house.

Get to the Real Picture

Most agents ask what your must-haves and dealbreakers are and call it done. I ask the questions that get you thinking about things you hadn't considered yet — tradeoffs you didn't know you'd face, priorities you hadn't ranked, scenarios you hadn't pictured. If you're a couple, we work through this together, so you're both starting from the same page instead of finding out mid-search that you wanted different things. Once we've gone this deep, we essentially have a roadmap to your perfect home — the closest thing to a cheat code in this process. Instead of touring twenty homes hoping one clicks, the homes I send you already check most of the boxes. Your time matters, and this is how we protect it.

Search With a Plan

I build a targeted search around exactly what we've defined, and send you homes worth your time instead of a flood of listings that don't fit.

Tour With a System

We visit homes together using a consistent process, so after the fifth or sixth showing they don't all start blending together in your head.

Make a Competitive Offer

When you've found the one, I evaluate the market and draft an offer that protects your interests while giving you a real shot at winning it.

Negotiate and Execute the Contract

I walk you through every page of the contract so nothing surprises you, and negotiate on your behalf using real market data, not guesswork.

Inspect and Verify

We attend the home inspection together, and I help you decide what's worth negotiating and what isn't.

Close With Confidence

I prepare you for closing day well in advance, so it's paperwork and a handshake, not a scramble.

Ready When You Are

If you're thinking about buying a home, let's talk about what this actually looks like for you — no pressure, no obligation, just a real conversation about where you're starting from and what you need.
